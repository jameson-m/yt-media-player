yt-media-player · design mockups

Five directions for a tiny native macOS mini player for YouTube background music. Same brief, different bets on what should be front and center. Click a card to open it full size. Toggle light/dark with the ◐ button.

Selected direction

Immersive

Video fills the window; Liquid Glass controls float over it and fade when idle. Channel switching is a small overlaid chip, not a persistent panel.

Shared principles across all five

  • Liquid Glass — translucent layered surfaces with specular rims, concentric corner radii, controls floating above content rather than in chrome.
  • Favorites are channels — Prev/Next steps through saved streams, not YouTube's queue. Live status is polled and shown as a red dot.
  • URL in, nothing else — no built-in browsing. Paste a link (field or ⌘V) and it plays. Browse YouTube in a browser.
  • Minimum controls — play/pause, prev/next favorite, volume/mute, favorite, keep-on-top. Everything else hides behind "…".
  • Small by default — every layout survives shrinking; secondary UI drops first, play + volume drop last.
  • Native hooks — Now Playing / media keys, system volume HUD styling, SF Symbols, automatic light/dark.
