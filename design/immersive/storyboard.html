Interaction rules

  • Hover fade: controls fade in on mouse move, fade out 2s after it stops; the transition itself is 150ms.
  • Move the window by dragging anywhere on the video — there's no title bar.
  • Double-click the video toggles the audio-only pill layout.
  • Scroll anywhere over the video adjusts volume, with a brief HUD.
  • Space toggles play/pause; ↑ / ↓ adjust volume.
  • ← / → step to the previous/next live favorite, skipping offline ones.
  • The window resizes from any edge, aspect locked to 16:9, minimum 240×135.
  • Keep on Top uses a floating window level and hides from Mission Control.
  • Media keys and AirPods controls work via MPNowPlayingInfoCenter.
  • The app remembers the last stream, volume, and window position on relaunch.
  • Favorite live status is polled every 5 minutes (configurable in Settings).
