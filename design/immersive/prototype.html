How to poke at it
  • Move the mouse in and out of the window — controls fade after a couple seconds idle.
  • Space plays/pauses; ←/→ cycle live favorites; ↑/↓ adjust volume.
  • Click the channel chip (top right) to switch stations.
  • Double-click the video to toggle audio-only pill mode.
  • Use the toolbar above to try Large/Compact sizes, empty, ended, and loading states.
