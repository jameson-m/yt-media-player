Channel Dock — favorites first

Treats your saved streams like TV channels. A slim dock on the left lists favorites as avatars; a red dot means it's live right now, greyed-out means the channel isn't streaming. Click to switch, drag to reorder.
  • Toolbar field accepts a pasted URL (or a search term) — this is how new things get in. ⌘L focuses it from anywhere.
  • Transport bar is classic three-zone: now-playing · controls · volume. Familiar to anyone who's used Music.app.
  • Audio-only toggle (top right of video) collapses the player, leaving just dock + transport — a very short, wide window.
  • Live status is polled every few minutes for each favorite; a notification is optional when a favorite goes live.
