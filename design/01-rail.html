Rail — the sketch, made native

Closest to the drawing. A single floating glass rail on the left holds volume (vertical) and transport; the video is the hero and everything else lives as small glass buttons on top of it.
  • Prev / Next step through your favorites, not the YouTube queue — this is a channel changer.
  • Heart / Link / Pin (favorite, paste URL, keep-on-top) sit over the video and fade when the mouse leaves.
  • Caption pill shows title, channel, live viewers, and an audio-activity meter so you know it's playing even when the window is tiny.
  • Window is resizable down to ~300×200; below that the caption hides and the rail shrinks to volume + play.
