Immersive — video is the window

No chrome at all. The video bleeds edge to edge behind the traffic lights, and every control floats on glass over it. Move the mouse away and everything but the picture fades out, so at rest it's just a small living thumbnail on your desk.
  • Bottom bar is one continuous glass capsule: transport · volume · favorite · more. Below ~360px wide the volume slider collapses to a mute button that pops a vertical slider.
  • Channel chip (top right) shows what's playing; clicking it drops a favorites menu with live dots, so switching stations never leaves this view.
  • More (…) holds the rarely used stuff: paste URL, keep on top, audio-only mode, quality.
  • Double-click the video to toggle a true mini mode (just the bar + a 16:9 sliver).
