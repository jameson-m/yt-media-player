Now Playing — audio first

Admits what this really is: a radio. The video is treated as album art — a frozen frame of the stream, with an optional "Show video" toggle that swaps in the live player. Portrait, narrow, tucks neatly beside a code editor.
  • Layout borrows from Music.app's Mini Player: art · title · big transport · volume · then favorites.
  • Favorites strip shows live dots; the selected one gets a ring. Prev/Next steps through this strip.
  • Paste a URL anywhere in the window (⌘V) and it just starts playing — no field needed. A "+" adds the current stream to favorites.
  • Cheapest on CPU: audio-only by default, video decoding only when you ask for it.
