Menu Bar — no window at all

The most "out of the way" option. The app lives as a menu bar extra (animated bars while playing). Clicking it drops a glass popover with now-playing, transport, volume, your favorites with live status, and a paste field. Close it and it's gone.
  • Popover is the whole app for 90% of use. Favorites are a real list with names and viewer counts, not just avatars.
  • Floating pill is an optional companion — a tiny keep-on-top capsule with art, title, play/pause, and a volume pop. Drag it anywhere; it can be turned off in settings.
  • "Show window" (footer) opens a proper video window when you actually want to watch — could reuse mockup 02's immersive view.
  • Media keys (F8 / AirPods) drive play/pause since it registers with Now Playing on macOS.
